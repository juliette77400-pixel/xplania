Xplania — AI travel companion

Xplania plans, budgets, packs, tracks and journals your trips with an AI that gives tips specific to your destination, never generic advice. Xplania est un compagnon de voyage IA : itinéraires personnalisés, budget réaliste, valise intelligente, formalités visa, suivi en direct et carnet de voyage.

Tools

  • Budget guide — per-day budget, expense tracking, currency conversion, AI saving tips.
  • Packing guide — packing list adapted to destination, weather, luggage type and activities.
  • Visa & vaccines guide — entry formalities, vaccines and safety advice from official sources.
  • Mood Explorer — places recommended from how you feel.
  • Discover — local places, map and natural-language search.
  • Live trip tracking — map, timeline, check-ins, shareable link.
  • Travel journal (Carnet) — days, photos, notes, AI-written story, PDF export, public page.
  • Traveler profile quiz — swipe cards to get a traveler badge and recommended tools.
