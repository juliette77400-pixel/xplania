- AI safety: every AI prompt must include SAFETY_RULE_* from supabase/functions/_shared/prompts.ts (via brandHeader or explicitly) — why: no illegal/dangerous/disrespectful recommendations.
- Cultural guide: pre-written country sheets live in src/data/culture-guide.ts; other pillars are AI-generated one at a time by the culture-guide function and cached in culture_guide_cache (server-write only) — why: keep AI cost to one generation per country/pillar/language.
- Validate travel basics before advancing or generating, and await trip persistence before clearing the draft; retry failed persistence without regenerating AI — why: prevent invalid requests, silent data loss and unnecessary AI usage.
- Map authentication errors through the shared auth-errors helper — why: sign-in and password recovery must display consistent localized messages.
- Keep the quiz introduction dismissal local to each page visit and gate quiz keyboard shortcuts and completion redirects until dismissal — why: returning visitors must see the overview without accidentally answering or being redirected.
- Offer explicit confirmed quiz restart on the introduction for completed travelers; keep ordinary entry redirecting to the app and block answers until the saved profile is loaded and incomplete — why: retakes must be intentional, not overwrite a completed profile accidentally.
- The root landing page renders directly (no JourneyIntro gate); its CTAs send signed-out visitors to signup with next=/preparation and signed-in users to /app — why: the problem, solution and signup must be understood in seconds.
- Auth emails (signup, recovery) are bilingual FR/EN: the device language is saved at signup in `user_metadata.locale` (via `browserLang()`), the auth-email-hook reads it from the webhook payload and falls back to French — why: emails must match the user's computer language without storing new tables.
- Module visibility lives only in src/config/featureFlags.ts: routes go through `gate()` in App.tsx (hidden → redirect to PREPARATION_HOME, lazy page never rendered) and every link/menu/shortcut filters with `isPathEnabled()` — why: hidden beta modules must come back by flipping one flag, with no dead links.
- Dashboard module progress is read through src/lib/prep-progress.ts (per-destination summaries written by each tool) — why: the dashboard must not depend on itinerary/AI data or on another module's internals.
- The preparation questionnaire (/preparation, src/pages/PrepQuiz.tsx) is the only way to create a trip; its rules live in src/lib/prep-quiz.ts and answers are stored in trips.form_data, edited through PrepTripEditDialog — why: the four guides share one source of trip data and never ask twice.
- Users without a trip are redirected from /app to /preparation; /preparation redirects users who already have a trip unless ?new=1 — why: nobody is forced to redo the questionnaire.
- Beta funnel events (quiz_step, dashboard_viewed, guide_started, guide_action) are allowed by the analytics_events check constraint; add new event names there and in analytics.ts together — why: inserts with unknown names are rejected silently.
