- Subdirectories may have their own AGENTS.md (e.g. supabase/functions/): read it before changing files there.
- Satisfaction survey answers live in `satisfaction_surveys` (insert/read own, admins read all); shown as a dismissible card on /app and aggregated in /admin/stats — why: real numbers for school pilot reports.
- Culture guide: hand-written sheets in src/data/culture-guide.ts (+ culture-sheets-more.ts); the culture-guide function generates and caches in culture_guide_cache (server-write only) one AI pillar per country/language (counted on the `culture` tries), the shared daily gesture (key per country/path/destination-local date) and example/nuance for hand-written tips (key per hash of tip texts), with the country name derived server-side; daily/enrich use no try — why: bounded AI cost, and callers cannot poison what other students read.
- Validate travel basics before advancing or generating, and await trip persistence before clearing the draft; retry failed persistence without regenerating AI — why: prevent invalid requests, silent data loss and unnecessary AI usage.
- Map authentication errors through the shared auth-errors helper — why: sign-in and password recovery must display consistent localized messages.
- Quiz introduction dismissal is local to each page visit; keyboard shortcuts and completion redirects wait for it — why: returning visitors must not answer or be redirected by accident.
- Completed travelers restart the quiz only through a confirmed button on the introduction; answers stay blocked until the saved profile is loaded and incomplete — why: retakes must never overwrite a completed profile by accident.
- The root landing page renders directly (no JourneyIntro gate); its CTAs send signed-out visitors straight to the questionnaire (/preparation) and signed-in users to /app — why: the problem, solution and a first result must come before any signup.
- Auth emails (signup, recovery) are bilingual FR/EN: the device language is saved at signup in `user_metadata.locale` (via `browserLang()`), the auth-email-hook reads it from the webhook payload and falls back to French — why: emails must match the user's computer language without storing new tables.
- Module visibility lives only in src/config/featureFlags.ts: routes go through `gate()` in App.tsx (hidden → redirect to PREPARATION_HOME, lazy page never rendered) and every link/menu/shortcut filters with `isPathEnabled()` — why: hidden beta modules must come back by flipping one flag, with no dead links.
- Dashboard module progress is read through src/lib/prep-progress.ts (per-destination summaries written by each tool) — why: the dashboard must not depend on itinerary/AI data or on another module's internals.
- The preparation questionnaire (/preparation, src/pages/PrepQuiz.tsx) is the only way to create a trip and is open to visitors: guest answers stay in a browser draft, end on a preview from src/lib/prep-preview.ts, and become the trip right after signup (next=/preparation?resume=1); rules live in src/lib/prep-quiz.ts, answers in trips.form_data, edited through PrepTripEditDialog — why: students see value before creating an account, and the tools share one source of trip data.
- A finished guest draft (hasCompleteGuestDraft) also travels with the signup as user_metadata.prep_draft (cleaned by cleanFinishedAnswers, cleared once the trip exists, never duplicated or overwritten by autosave); /auth without `next` sends its owner to PREP_RESUME_PATH — why: answers survive another device and any sign-up entry point.
- Users without a trip are redirected from /app to /preparation; /preparation redirects users who already have a trip unless ?new=1 — why: nobody is forced to redo the questionnaire.
- Beta funnel events (quiz_step, dashboard_viewed, guide_started, guide_action) are allowed by the analytics_events check constraint; add new event names there and in analytics.ts together — why: inserts with unknown names are rejected silently.
- Housing assistant: rules live in src/lib/housing.ts, hand-checked links in src/data/housing-resources.ts (never AI-generated), saved homes and checked steps in housing_options / housing_checklist (own rows only, insert limited to own trips); the search status is trips.form_data.housingStatus; budget sync writes the Budget guide's local state and remembers its last contribution per trip — why: no invented resources, no data leaks between students, and no double-counted costs.
- Budget amounts are never replaced without a confirmation dialog listing the changes, and the estimate is never stretched to match the student's budget — why: students must keep control of their numbers and see a real margin.
- The dashboard "what's left to prepare" list is built by src/lib/next-steps.ts from what each tool saved (Visa checklist, Budget state, housing status, progress) — why: one prioritised to-do list without coupling the dashboard to tool internals or calling AI.
- Visa for non-French nationals: the AI answer is shown only as a lead marked "to confirm", next to the destination's hand-checked official entry site from src/data/entry-sources.ts; post-login redirects go through safeNextPath (src/lib/safe-redirect.ts) — why: never present an AI guess as an official rule, and never send a student to an outside site after sign-in.
- Human review of official summaries happens on /admin/sources (official_info_cache.reviewed_at, admin-only RLS, column-level UPDATE grant); official-sources clears the review whenever it rebuilds a summary — why: a "reviewed" badge must always match the exact text that was re-read.
- School pricing lives only in src/lib/school-pricing.ts and reuses the student pack prices from the Offres page — why: one price list, the school total can never drift from the student price.
- Public country guides live at /preparer and /preparer/:slug, built only from src/data/destination-pages.ts, the pre-written culture sheets, entry-sources and housing-resources; each guide needs a META entry and a sitemap line (tested) — why: SEO pages must never show AI or invented figures and must stay indexable.
- Only one floating button exists (HelpButton "Aide"); the assistant chat and the AI-report form open through src/lib/help-events.ts, and pages listed in TOOL_PIP_ROUTES open their own assistant from that event instead of a launcher — why: stacked floating buttons hid page content.
- The guided tour opens only on PREPARATION_HOME and only after the cookie choice — why: first visits must never stack two overlays.
- Budget, Visa and Culture work without an account (src/config/open-tools.ts); Valise and Logement keep data on the account, so visitors get the AccountNeeded explanation via ProtectedRoute `explain` instead of a redirect — why: access rules must be visible and explained, not surprising.
- Journal photos/audio store only their file path; links are re-signed for a short time on every load through src/lib/journal-media.ts, and public viewers can sign only while the journal is public — why: turning sharing off must cut access quickly.
- Scores are server-derived: explore node points come from a database trigger, explore_progress is written only by triggers, and leaderboard-xp caps self-reported activity per kind and per day using server-set timestamps — why: users must not award themselves points.
- Every `supabase.functions.invoke()` goes through src/lib/invoke-retry.ts (installed in main.tsx), which retries only 503 BOOT_ERROR start-up failures — why: idle functions fail to start when a whole class clicks at once, and that failure uses no AI or try.
- The quota banner reads the server count through useQuota; the client LIMITS in usage-quota.ts are only an offline fallback mirroring get_quota_limit — why: a stale local limit showed students as out of tries.
- Admin rights are granted only by user id in public.user_roles (no e-mail-based triggers); consume_quota/get_quota_status exempt admins server-side — why: an e-mail match (with a typo) could hand admin to a stranger.
- Valise activities and transport are saved per trip in trips.form_data (valiseActivities, valiseActivityOther, valiseTransport; ids in src/lib/valise-activities.ts) and merged into the latest form_data before update — why: choices must come back on every visit without overwriting other answers.
- Every external link shown to students must be listed in supabase/functions/_shared/checked-links.ts (a test enforces it); the daily link-check job writes link_checks, and the UI shows the automatic test date separately from the hand-review date — why: a link that opens never proves its content is still right, and a failed test must never hide behind an older date.
- The beta promo code is checked only by redeem_promo_code (codes in private.promo_codes, never in the client) and writes public.beta_access; consume_quota/get_quota_status give holders unlimited tries until expires_at, never the admin role — why: a shared code must not open admin pages or be guessable from the app.
- Culture and Housing assistants call the tool-assistant function (context rebuilt server-side, see supabase/functions/AGENTS.md); one try of `culture` / `housing` per question; panel, question ideas, tries note and bold rendering live in src/components/shared/assistant — why: one consistent assistant per tool, no free unlimited AI.
- The display name is read and saved only through src/lib/data/profiles.ts (useDisplayName / useFirstName / useUpdateDisplayName); its rules live in src/lib/display-name.ts and the profiles trigger cleans every write the same way — why: a rename shows everywhere at once and never touches the e-mail or sign-in data.
- Saved destinations are displayed through placeName (src/lib/country-match.ts) so bare country names follow the interface language — why: trips typed in French must not show French country names in English.

- Visa checklist gaps are filled client-side by src/lib/visa-essentials.ts with i18n texts (never AI, figures or links) — why: an AI omission must never drop a step students cannot skip.
